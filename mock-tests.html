Japanese driving rules, true or false

556 true/false statements on the rules the written test checks, in plain English. Mark ○ if the statement is correct and × if it is wrong.

The conversion test is 50 questions, not 95. Japanese learners taking the full test for a new license (本免) answer 95 questions in 50 minutes. If you are converting a foreign license (外免切替), you answer 50 true/false questions in 30 minutes and need 45 right. Until October 2025 the conversion test had only 10 questions, mostly picture questions, and 7 right was a pass.

Vehicle types

Which class is which vehicle

A vehicle goes in the biggest class where it meets any one of the limits.

Not on page 17, but in the questions

Light vehicle

  • Bicycles, hand carts, rickshaws, horse-drawn carts
  • A horse being ridden counts too
  • No license; must obey signals

Trap: a light vehicle (bicycles, carts) is not a kei car (a small 660 cc car). A kei car is an ordinary car.

Who counts as a pedestrian

  • A person in a wheelchair, even an electric one
  • Someone pushing a bicycle
  • Someone pushing a motorcycle or moped with the engine off

Trap: pushing a two-wheeler with the engine running is not a pedestrian.

What your ordinary license covers

  • Ordinary cars (incl. kei cars, minicars, three-wheelers)
  • Small special vehicles
  • Mopeds
  • Not motorcycles, except 125 cc bikes limited to 4.0 kW, which count as mopeds since April 2025
  • Not trucks with 2 t+ load or 3,500 kg+ gross
