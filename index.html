From California to Japan

This guide was made while converting a California driver's license to a Japanese one, in 2026. California has no agreement with Japan, so the conversion takes both tests: the written test and the behind-the-wheel test. Everything on this site is what that preparation needed, after a first attempt that didn't go to plan.

Good to know before you start

Your US state may skip the tests

Licenses from seven states are converted without either test: Colorado, Hawaii, Maryland, Ohio, Oregon, Virginia and Washington. Indiana skips the driving test only. Every other state, California included, takes both tests.

Canada, the UK, Australia, New Zealand, South Korea, Taiwan and most of Western Europe are exempt too: 29 countries and regions in all.

Source: Tokyo Metropolitan Police, updated November 2026.

The written test is 50 questions, not 95

Japanese learners sit a 95-question test for a new license. For a conversion it is 50 true/false questions in 30 minutes, and you need 45 right.

Until October 2025 it was only 10 questions, mostly pictures, and 7 right was a pass. The mock tests on this site follow the new format.

The tests got much harder in 2025

  • In Aichi, the written-test pass rate fell from 94% in 2024 to 35% in October 2025, the first month of the new rules.
  • Nationally, only about 1 in 7 candidates passed the driving test in the six months that followed. Failing the first time is normal.
  • In 2024, a record 75,905 foreign residents got a Japanese license this way.

Paperwork that catches people out

  • You must show you lived in the country that issued your license for at least 3 months after it was issued. Passport stamps are the usual proof.
  • If you renewed your US license recently, the card shows the new date. Bring your DMV driving record showing when you were first licensed.
  • Booking the tests takes time: after you hand in your documents, the center calls you to schedule an interview, and only after the interview can you book your test day.
  • Since October 2025, tourists can no longer convert: you need a Japanese residence record (住民票).
  • Your first Japanese license has a green band. It can turn blue or gold at your first renewal.
