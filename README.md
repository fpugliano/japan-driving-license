# Japan driving license conversion: free study tools

Free, unofficial study pages for foreigners converting an overseas driving license in Japan (外免切替, gaimen kirikae).

- **[Mock tests](mock-tests.html)**: timed 50-question true/false written tests (pass mark 45/50), a signs and signals lesson, and vehicle classes explained with photos.
- **[Behind-the-wheel test day](test-day.html)**: an animated example test course with every trap marked, the routines examiners score, and the Japanese instructions you will hear. Includes an English rules sheet ([PDF](Behind-the-Wheel-Test-Rules.pdf)).

Not official. Rules, fees and procedures differ between test centers and can change, so always check with your own center. Scores are saved only in your browser.

## Image credits

Road sign images are the standard Japanese designs (Road Sign Order), taken from public-domain files on [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Road_signs_in_Japan).
